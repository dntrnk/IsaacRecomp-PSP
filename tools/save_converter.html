Isaac save converter

Move your progress between the original Flash version of The Binding of Isaac and IsaacRecomp, in either direction, for all three versions: the original game, Wrath of the Lamb and Eternal Edition. Everything runs in your browser; your files never leave your computer.

Flash to IsaacRecomp

Take the Flash game's so.sol and get the save file IsaacRecomp reads.

IsaacRecomp to Flash

Take isaac_og_save.sol, isaac_save.sol or isaac_eternal_save.sol and get a so.sol for the original Flash game.

Before you start

Close the game first (it rewrites its save when it exits) and keep a copy of the save you are about to replace.

Where the files are

IsaacRecomp, one file per version (isaac_og_save.sol original, isaac_save.sol Wrath of the Lamb, isaac_eternal_save.sol Eternal Edition):

  • Windows and Linux: the saves folder next to the program (IsaacRecomp.exe / isaac), beside the tools folder this page came in
  • Android: IsaacRecomp/saves/ in the internal storage
  • Nintendo Switch: sd:/switch/isaac/saves/

Versions before 1.4.0 kept them elsewhere (before 1.3.1 on PC: %APPDATA%\IsaacRecomp\WrathOfTheLamb\ or ~/.local/share/IsaacRecomp/WrathOfTheLamb/; on Android and Switch, directly in IsaacRecomp/ and sd:/switch/isaac/). The current version copies them into saves by itself.

Flash game (Steam): so.sol lives in Flash Player's shared objects folder, usually %APPDATA%\Macromedia\Flash Player\#SharedObjects\<random name>\localhost\so.sol (search that folder for so.sol if it isn't there).

What carries over

Stats, collected items, secrets and their unlock order, beaten bosses, completion marks, hard mode and the lefty/AZERTY control options. Flash-only display settings (window size, resolution) are left out when going to IsaacRecomp, and the Flash game picks its defaults for them when going back.
